# RBI Fair Practices Code for NBFCs
## Based on RBI Master Direction — Non-Banking Financial Companies (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2016
### (Simplified Reference for Internal Credit Underwriting System)

---

## A. Loan Application & Processing

### A.1 Loan Application Form
- All loan applications must clearly disclose all fees, charges, and terms
- A receipt with unique application number must be issued to borrowers
- Lenders must communicate the loan decision (approve/reject) within 30 days of complete documentation

### A.2 Assessment of Credit Requirement
- NBFCs must use standardised credit assessment methodologies
- Income assessment must rely on verifiable documents (salary slips, ITR, GST returns)
- NBFCs must not grant loans based on unverified oral income declarations

### A.3 Rejection Communication
- On rejection, the NBFC must communicate the primary reason for rejection in writing
- Applicants must be informed of their right to re-apply after 90 days
- Rejection letters must cite specific policy clause(s) that were not met

---

## B. Interest Rate Policy

### B.1 Transparency
- Interest rates must be communicated as Annual Percentage Rate (APR) inclusive of all fees
- Interest rate must not exceed the Board-approved ceiling (Board to review annually)
- Rate of interest charged must be communicated in loan sanction letter

### B.2 Differential Pricing
- Risk-based pricing is permitted but must be documented in the credit policy
- The basis for differential interest rates must be disclosed to borrowers
- NBFCs must not charge different rates to borrowers of the same risk profile

### B.3 Prepayment
- No prepayment penalty for floating-rate personal loans
- Prepayment penalty for fixed-rate loans must not exceed 2% of outstanding principal

---

## C. Eligible Borrower Categories

### C.1 Individual Borrowers
- Any Indian resident individual aged 18–65 years (NBFC-specific policies may be more restrictive)
- NRIs may borrow subject to FEMA regulations and enhanced KYC

### C.2 MSME Borrowers
- Entities registered under MSME Act with valid Udyam registration
- Turnover not exceeding ₹250 crore for manufacturing; ₹100 crore for services
- Loan amounts above ₹50 lakh for MSME require audited financials

### C.3 Excluded Categories
- Wilful defaulters as per RBI/CRILC database
- Entities under NCLT/IBC proceedings
- Borrowers with fraud classification by any regulated lender

---

## D. KYC and Anti-Money Laundering

### D.1 KYC Requirements
- Aadhaar-based eKYC is acceptable for individuals
- PAN card is mandatory for all loans above ₹50,000
- Form 60 acceptable in lieu of PAN only for small-value loans (up to ₹10,000)

### D.2 Beneficial Ownership
- For corporate/partnership borrowers, beneficial ownership (>25% stake) must be identified
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) require enhanced due diligence

### D.3 Address Verification
- Address on KYC documents must be verifiable via Aadhaar database or utility bills
- PO Box addresses are not accepted as primary address

---

## E. Grievance Redressal

### E.1 Internal Mechanism
- NBFC must have a designated Nodal Officer for complaints
- Complaints must be resolved within 30 days of receipt
- Escalation to RBI Ombudsman available if unresolved

### E.2 Adverse Action Notice
- Borrowers denied credit must receive an Adverse Action Notice within 7 days
- The notice must cite the specific reason: CIBIL score, FOIR breach, documentation gap, etc.
- Borrowers have the right to request their CIBIL report free of charge once per year

---

## F. Disclosure Requirements

### F.1 Sanction Letter
The loan sanction letter must include:
- Loan amount, interest rate (APR), tenure
- EMI schedule
- All fees: processing fee, documentation charges, stamp duty
- Insurance premium (if applicable; must be clearly stated as optional)
- Penal interest clause for late payment

### F.2 Annual Statement
- Borrowers must receive an annual statement of account
- Outstanding principal, interest paid YTD, charges levied must be itemised

---

## G. Recovery Practices

### G.1 Recovery Agents
- NBFC must not employ recovery agents who use coercive or intimidatory tactics
- Calls to borrowers must be made only between 07:00 and 19:00 hours
- Third-party collection agents must adhere to IBA guidelines on debt collection

### G.2 No Harassment
- Contacting family members / employers without borrower consent is prohibited
- Public shaming or social media identification of defaulters is strictly prohibited

---

## H. Credit Information Reporting

### H.1 CIBIL/Bureau Reporting
- All disbursements and payments must be reported to at least one CIBIL-authorised bureau monthly
- Incorrect bureau data must be corrected within 30 days of borrower complaint

### H.2 Derogatory Marks
- DPD reporting must be accurate; incorrect DPD flags are a regulatory violation
- NBFCs must provide a dispute resolution mechanism for credit bureau disputes
