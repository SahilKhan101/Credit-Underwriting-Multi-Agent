# Internal Credit Policy — Version 2.3
## FinEdge Capital Private Limited (NBFC-MFI)
### Effective Date: April 1, 2025

---

## Section 1: Eligibility Criteria

### 1.1 Age
- Minimum applicant age: 21 years
- Maximum applicant age: 60 years at loan maturity
- Age at maturity = Current age + Loan tenure in years

### 1.2 Employment & Income
**Salaried Applicants:**
- Minimum employment continuity: 6 months with current employer; 2 years total employment history
- Minimum net monthly income: ₹20,000 for Tier-1 cities; ₹15,000 for Tier-2/3
- Eligible income: Salary credited to bank account only; cash salary not accepted

**Self-Employed / Business Owners:**
- Minimum 2 years of ITR filing history
- ITR income to be averaged over last 2 years for income computation
- Bank credit turnover must be ≥ 3× declared income
- GST registration mandatory for loan amounts above ₹10 lakh

**Professionals (CA, Doctor, Engineer, Lawyer):**
- Minimum 1 year post-qualification practice
- Income per professional income certificate or latest IT assessment

### 1.3 CIBIL / Credit Score
- Minimum CIBIL score: 650 for personal loans; 620 for MSME loans
- Applicants with no credit history (New-to-Credit): eligible up to ₹2 lakh with enhanced documentation
- CIBIL score below 550: ineligible regardless of other parameters

### 1.4 Loan Purpose Eligibility
Eligible purposes:
- Home renovation / repairs
- Education (own or immediate family)
- Medical emergency
- Vehicle purchase (two-wheeler or four-wheeler)
- Business expansion / working capital (MSME)
- Personal (general consumption) — capped at ₹5 lakh

Ineligible purposes:
- Speculative investments (stocks, crypto)
- Repayment of existing unsecured loans (balance transfer to other NBFCs without RBI approval)
- Real estate purchases (refer to home loan product)

## Section 2: Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)

### 2.1 FOIR Definition
FOIR = (Total existing monthly EMI obligations + proposed new EMI) ÷ Gross Monthly Income

### 2.2 FOIR Limits
| Segment | Maximum FOIR |
|---|---|
| Salaried, income ≥ ₹50,000/month | 55% |
| Salaried, income ₹20,000–₹49,999/month | 50% |
| Self-Employed | 45% |
| MSME / Business | 50% |

### 2.3 FOIR Overrides
- Applicants with CIBIL ≥ 800 may receive a 5% FOIR relaxation (subject to credit committee approval)
- Applicants with FOIR > 60% are ineligible regardless of CIBIL score

## Section 3: Loan-to-Income (LTI) Limits

### 3.1 Maximum Loan Amount
| Income Band | Maximum Loan (multiple of annual income) |
|---|---|
| ₹20,000–₹40,000/month | 10× annual monthly income |
| ₹40,001–₹80,000/month | 15× annual monthly income |
| > ₹80,000/month       | 18× annual monthly income |

### 3.2 Absolute Limits
- Personal loans: Maximum ₹25 lakh
- MSME loans: Maximum ₹1 crore (subject to business vintage and turnover)
- Education loans: Maximum ₹10 lakh (unsecured); ₹30 lakh (secured against property)

---

## Section 4: Bank Statement Requirements

### 4.1 Statement Period
- Minimum 6 months of bank statements required
- 12 months preferred for loan amounts > ₹10 lakh

### 4.2 Average Bank Balance (ABB)
- ABB must be ≥ 1.5× the proposed monthly EMI
- Accounts with persistent minimum-balance violations are flagged for enhanced review

### 4.3 Bounce Rate
- Maximum permissible bounce rate: 3% of transactions
- More than 2 bounced EMI payments in 12 months: ineligible

### 4.4 Salary Credits
- Salary must be credited electronically (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) at least 5 of the last 6 months
- Cash salary deposits do not qualify as verifiable income

---

## Section 5: Derogatory Marks

### 5.1 Automatic Disqualification
The following result in automatic rejection without exception:
- Any DPD 90+ (Days Past Due) in the last 24 months
- Written-off or settled accounts in the last 36 months
- Wilful defaulter tag from any lender or DRT
- Fraud flag from CIBIL or CRIF

### 5.2 Conditional Review
The following require senior credit officer review:
- DPD 30–89 in the last 12 months (more than 2 instances)
- More than 5 credit enquiries in the last 6 months

---

## Section 6: Fraud Prevention

### 6.1 Velocity Check
- More than 3 loan applications across lenders in 30 days: automatic fraud flag; refer to credit committee
- More than 6 applications in 90 days: automatic rejection

### 6.2 Income Verification
- Stated income must be within 20% of estimated income (derived from employment, industry, location data)
- Cash income claims above ₹30,000/month require additional ITR and business proof

### 6.3 Address & Identity
- KYC documents must show consistent address; PIN code must correspond to declared state
- Name on Aadhaar must match PAN exactly (minor variations acceptable with supporting affidavit)

## Section 7: Interest Rates & Fees

### 7.1 Interest Rate Bands (Risk-Based Pricing)
| Credit Score Band | Interest Rate (p.a.) |
|---|---|
| 750+ (Auto Approve) | 10.99% – 13.99% |
| 650–749 (Approve with conditions) | 14.00% – 18.99% |
| 550–649 (Refer) | 19.00% – 24.00% |
| Below 550 | Not eligible |

### 7.2 Processing Fees
- Personal loans: 1.5%–2.5% of loan amount + GST
- MSME loans: 1.0%–2.0% of loan amount + GST
- No fee charged for rejected applications

---

## Section 8: Decision Authority

| Credit Score | Decision Authority |
|---|---|
| ≥ 750 | System (Auto Approve) |
| 650–749 | Branch Credit Manager |
| 550–649 | Regional Credit Head |
| < 550 | System (Auto Reject) |

All auto-approvals are subject to post-disbursement audit sampling (10% random sample).
